Tiramisu in Kraków, made every day in Krowodrza.

COPPA was built around two things: specialty coffee and tiramisu. Tiramisu is the main product here and the idea the whole place grew from.

Served in a coppa

The name comes from the Italian word for a goblet. From the start we wanted to serve tiramisu as its own, finished dessert in a small cup. The coppa became the most recognisable part of the concept.

Every day, in-house

We make it ourselves, every single day. Tiramisu gives us an Italian starting point, but we do not copy an Italian café one to one. That is where hojicha, pralines, seasonal fruit and modern specialty coffee come in.

Kasia and Tomek

COPPA is run by its owners, not a chain or an investor. Kasia is responsible for the tiramisu and creates every new flavour, Tomek runs the bar.

Krowodrza, close to the centre

We are in Krowodrza, an 8-minute walk from Plac Inwalidów and about 25 minutes from the Main Square, away from the tourist crowds. That is part of the character of the place. We want to be a café people come back to, not a stop on a weekend route. Open since August 2026.

A house espresso and a second grinder that keeps changing.

Espresso
Our house espresso comes from the Roastains roastery. We go for a flavour that works black and with milk.
Second grinder
We rotate the beans regularly and show fruitier, less typical profiles, Ethiopia or Costa Rica for example. That is where our funky flat white comes from.
Roasters
We work only with specialty coffee. Beyond the house espresso we rotate coffees from different roasters, very often Sweden's Gringo Nordic Coffee Roasters.
Milk
Regular, lactose-free, oat or pea. Every option at no extra charge.

The things people ask most.

Where can I get good tiramisu in Kraków?

At COPPA, Mazowiecka 60, in Krowodrza. We make our tiramisu on site every day and serve it in a coppa. Classic and pistachio are always on, plus seasonal flavours.

Which tiramisu flavours do you have?

Always on: classic and pistachio. Seasonal right now: Earl Grey, hojicha, limoncello, white peach with lemon thyme, hazelnut praline, matcha with raspberry, plum with crumble. Seasonal flavours rotate; the current counter is above and on Instagram.

Is there a coffee-free or alcohol-free tiramisu?

Alcohol-free: classic, pistachio, Earl Grey, hojicha, limoncello and matcha with raspberry. Coffee-free: Earl Grey, hojicha, limoncello and matcha with raspberry. Ask at the counter about the others.

Can I take tiramisu away?

Yes. Usually 3 or 4 flavours a day are available to take away, not every flavour. Ask at the counter which ones are on today.

What coffee do you serve?

Specialty only. The house espresso is from Roastains; the second grinder rotates fruitier single origins such as Ethiopia or Costa Rica. We also often work with Gringo Nordic Coffee Roasters from Sweden. On the standard menu: espresso, cortado, cappuccino, flat white, latte, iced latte, batch brew, hand brew, cold brew, orange espresso and espresso tonic.

Do you have plant milk?

Yes. Regular, lactose-free, oat or pea, all at no extra charge.

Does COPPA have a pumpkin spice latte?

Yes. In autumn the menu has Pumpkin Spice made with our homemade syrup of roasted pumpkin and warm spices with a hint of orange. Also Iced Banana Bread, Iced Apple Pie Latte, Banana Bread Latte, White Vanilla Matcha, Berry Cocoa, Dark Chocolate Mocha and Oat Cinnamon Hojicha with oat milk.

Does COPPA serve sandwiches?

Yes, ciabatta sandwiches: salami piccante with ricotta and chilli honey, mortadella with stracciatella and pistachio, and a vegetarian one with olives, sun-dried tomatoes and stracciatella.

Is there WiFi?

Yes, the network is COPPA GUEST. The password is in the At the café section and at the counter.

Where are you and how do I get there?

Mazowiecka 60, 30-019 Kraków, in Krowodrza: an 8-minute walk from Plac Inwalidów, 10 minutes from Park Krakowski and Nowy Kleparz, about 25 minutes from the Main Square. Open the route in Google Maps or Apple Maps in the Contact section.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday 8:00-19:00, Saturday and Sunday 9:30-18:00.

Who runs COPPA?

Kasia and Tomek, the owners. Kasia is responsible for the tiramisu and creates all the new flavours, Tomek runs the bar. We opened in August 2026.
