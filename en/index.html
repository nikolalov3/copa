02 · The place

A café in Krowodrza, not in the centre. On purpose.

COPPA was built around two things: specialty coffee and tiramisu. Tiramisu is the main product here and the idea the whole place grew from. We make it ourselves, on site, every day.

The name comes from the Italian word for a goblet. From the start we wanted to serve tiramisu as its own, finished dessert in a small cup, not scooped from a large tray. The coppa became the most recognisable part of the concept.

COPPA is run by Kasia and Tomek, the owners. There is no chain and no investor behind it. Kasia is responsible for the tiramisu and creates every new flavour, Tomek runs the bar.

Tiramisu gives us an Italian starting point, but we do not copy an Italian café one to one. That is where hojicha, pralines, seasonal fruit and modern specialty coffee come in. The menu is meant to change with the season and with what we find interesting.

We are in Krowodrza, outside Kraków's tourist centre, and that is part of the character of the place. We want to be a café people come back to, not a stop on a weekend route. Open since August 2026.

03 · Coffee

A house espresso and a second grinder that keeps changing.

Espresso
Our house espresso comes from the Roastains roastery. We go for a flavour that works black and with milk.
Second grinder
We rotate the beans regularly and show fruitier, less typical profiles, Ethiopia or Costa Rica for example. That is where our funky flat white comes from.
Roasters
We work only with specialty coffee. Beyond the house espresso we rotate coffees from different roasters, very often Sweden's Gringo Nordic Coffee Roasters.
Milk
Regular, lactose-free, oat or pea. Every option at no extra charge.

05 · Questions

The things people ask most.

Where can I get good tiramisu in Kraków?

At COPPA, Mazowiecka 60, in Krowodrza. We make our tiramisu on site every day and serve it in a coppa. Classic, pistachio, Earl Grey and hojicha are always on, plus seasonal flavours.

Which tiramisu flavours do you have?

Always on: classic, pistachio, Earl Grey, hojicha. Seasonal right now: limoncello, white peach with lemon thyme, hazelnut praline, matcha with raspberry. Seasonal flavours rotate; the current counter is above and on Instagram.

Is there a coffee-free or alcohol-free tiramisu?

All our tiramisu are alcohol-free. Coffee-free: Earl Grey, hojicha, limoncello and matcha with raspberry.

Can I take tiramisu away?

Yes, every tiramisu can be packed to go.

What coffee do you serve?

Specialty only. The house espresso is from Roastains; the second grinder rotates fruitier single origins such as Ethiopia or Costa Rica. We also often work with Gringo Nordic Coffee Roasters from Sweden.

Do you have plant milk?

Yes. Regular, lactose-free, oat or pea, all at no extra charge.

Is there WiFi?

Yes, the network is COPPA GUEST. The password is in the At the café section and at the counter.

Where are you and how do I get there?

Mazowiecka 60, 30-019 Kraków, in Krowodrza, outside the tourist centre. Open the route in Google Maps or Apple Maps in the Contact section.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday 8:00-19:00, Saturday and Sunday 9:30-18:00.

Who runs COPPA?

Kasia and Tomek, the owners. Kasia is responsible for the tiramisu and creates all the new flavours, Tomek runs the bar. We opened in August 2026.
